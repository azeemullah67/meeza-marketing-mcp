# Example prompts

## Getting started
- "Which marketing accounts are connected?"

## Campaign analysis
- "Analyze my advertising campaigns for the last seven days."
- "Show my top 10 Meta campaigns by spend last month with CTR, CPC and CPI."
- "Break down my Meta campaign X by age and gender."

## Country comparison
- "Compare Pakistan, Philippines and the United States based on CPI."
- "Which countries have the cheapest installs on Google Ads this month?"

## Budget optimization
- "Which campaigns are spending money without generating installs?"
- "Based on my current campaign performance, recommend where I should invest my next $100. Separate the observed data from your recommendations."

## Acquisition analysis
- "Compare Meta Ads and Google Ads acquisition performance for the last 30 days."
- "Compare ad-attributed installs with Play Console store installs for last month. What share might be organic?"

## App quality
- "Are my crash or ANR rates above Google Play's bad-behavior thresholds?"
- "Which app versions have the highest crash rate over the last 28 days?"
- "Summarize this week's 1–2 star reviews and group them by theme."

## Retention (needs Google Analytics 4 / Firebase or Adjust)
- "Compare D1 and D7 retention by acquisition country."
